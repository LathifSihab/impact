[ About IMPACT ]

We don't build programs. We build foundations.

IMPACT is not a camp organisation and not a sports club. We build experiences where young people aged 8 to 25 work on the foundations that stay with them for life — guided by people who are experts in their field.

Why we exist

Because young people deserve better tools.

Young people grow up in a world full of stimuli, expectations and possibilities. School, sport, home and other environments teach them valuable tools for dealing with it.

IMPACT builds on that: through our programmes, young people learn to pause at who they already are — their identity, values and strengths, what gives them energy and joy, and what they need to feel good in themselves.

Founders

Meet the founders

Mirte Rens & Jean-Marc Mwema

Two founders, two backgrounds, one observation: young people are taught everything — except how to deal with themselves.

Founder

Jean-Marc Mwema

Elite sport — mindset — performance

For more than half my life my world has revolved around basketball. Around everything behind it: the discipline, the mindset, the environment, learning to handle pressure. The realisation that how you live off the court matters as much as what you do on it.

Throughout my career I have always felt that real impact goes further than statistics or talent alone. I was not always the best shooter, but I always tried to be someone who brought energy, carried people with him and had a positive impact on the team.

Basketball taught me how much habits, discipline, confidence and environment shape how someone grows. How sleep, nutrition, recovery, focus and mindset end up shaping who you become — as an athlete and as a person. That is what I want to pass on to young people now: to give them the head start I would have wanted myself.

Not just performing. Leaving something behind. Building a movement that genuinely gives today's young people something. That, to me, is real impact. x Jean-Marc
Founder

Mirte Rens

Ondernemerschap · brand & strategie · jongerencoach

In my day-to-day life I am an entrepreneur, creating brands, concepts and ideas from strategy, vision and instinct. I love building something from nothing, and thinking about how it can be strong as a business and genuinely create value.

I am also fascinated by how young people think, feel and discover the world. That became a training as a youth coach, and today I guide student entrepreneurs through their first steps in business. I see them blossom — but I also see what they carry: insecurity, pressure, decision stress, a lack of confidence.

The right guidance, environment and tools are enormously powerful. Building confidence, learning who you are, understanding how sleep, nutrition, energy and environment affect you, learning to handle pressure and to be kind to yourself. Those are the foundations — and yet we teach young people far too little about them. Within IMPACT my focus is the vision, the strategy, the brand and building out the concept.

I sometimes think about the girl I was. And about how much difference it would have made if someone had shown her how strong she already was. x Mirte
And so it began

And so this happened.

You meet someone. You talk. And somewhere in that conversation you feel it: okay, there is something here. Jean-Marc with his years in elite sport, his stories from the court, his drive to pass something on to the next generation. Mirte with her head full of concepts, her experience with young people and her talent for bringing ideas to life.

We complement each other where it counts. Jean-Marc knows from the inside what discipline and performance do to a person. Mirte knows how to turn that into an experience that stays with you. And together we simply enjoy what we are building. Always aimed at growth, always with fun and experience as the base.

Our foundations

Six foundations. One methodology.

The foundations are not a separate product or a separate activity. They are our method. Every Camp, Day, Retreat and Hosted Experience is built from these six.

01

Self-knowledge

Understanding who you are, how you work and what you need.

Self-knowledge is, to us, one of the most important foundations for personal growth. The better young people understand themselves, the better they handle choices, emotions, energy, pressure and challenges.

What we work on

  • How they react under pressure
  • What gives them energy, and what drains it
  • How their body and mindset work together
  • Their strengths, pitfalls and needs
  • Learning to listen to themselves
02

Healthy habits & lifestyle

Sleep, movement, nutrition, recovery and energy.

The way young people sleep, eat, move and recover has an enormous impact on how they feel, function and perform.

What we work on

  • Movement and body awareness
  • Healthy food
  • Sleep and recovery
  • Routines and structure
  • Managing energy and building habits that last
03

Resilience

Handling pressure, setbacks and uncertainty with strength and awareness.

Growing up brings pressure, uncertainty and challenges. The goal is to teach young people how to carry themselves through difficult moments.

What we work on

  • Handling pressure and expectations
  • Emotional resilience
  • Building confidence
  • Learning to deal with failure and setbacks
  • Flexibility and adaptability
04

Ownership

Taking responsibility for your growth, choices and direction.

Learning to take responsibility for yourself, your choices, your energy and your growth, from awareness and independence. We help young people realise that growth is not something someone else does for them.

What we work on

  • Taking initiative
  • Carrying responsibility
  • Building discipline
  • Learning to make deliberate choices
  • Learning to think independently
05

Human connection

Communicating, trusting and growing with others.

People do not grow alone. In a world that keeps getting more digital and more individual, we believe strongly in the power of real human connection.

What we work on

  • Learning to communicate
  • Collaborating and connecting
  • Building confidence
  • Feeling seen & seeing others
  • Experiencing connection offline
06

Talent & passion

Discovering what energizes you and what you want to build.

We make room to discover talents, interests and passions — without the pressure of already knowing who you are. As young people get closer to what genuinely energises them, direction and confidence follow by themselves.

What we work on

  • What gives them energy
  • What motivates them
  • Where their talents lie
  • What makes them curious
  • What they want to develop further

The system

One vision, four layers

The foundations and the vision stay the same. The medium, the intensity, the content and the approach change depending on who is in front of us and what we want to achieve.

01

IMPACT

The brand and the vision: helping young people build strong foundations for life.

02

Six fixed foundations

Self-knowledge · Healthy habits & lifestyle · Resilience · Ownership · Human connection · Talent & passion

03

Different life stages

8–14 · 14–18 · 18–25 — each with its own needs, and split further where that helps.

04

Formats & concrete activations

Days · Camps · Retreats · Community, also as a Hosted Experience — and within those, editions such as IMPACT Camp: Basketball Edition.

We are still early: not every format is equally worked out. The structure is built so a new Day, Camp, Retreat or Community initiative can be added without changing the logic of the site.
