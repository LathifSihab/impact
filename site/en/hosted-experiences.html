What it is

They host. IMPACT builds the experience.

A Hosted Experience is a Day or Camp we develop together with an external party. The host has their own community and reach; we bring our method, foundations, experts and experience design. The result is always bespoke — never an off-the-shelf package.

The host is the face and the organiser of the experience: they usually already have their own community and take on ticketing, communication, marketing and the venue. IMPACT adds its own team, the right experts and a programme built for that audience. That way we do not have to build every community ourselves or produce every event end to end.

This page deliberately sits under both EVENTS and PARTNER WITH US. To a participant or parent, a Hosted Experience is a kind of event. To a club or organisation, it is a way of working with IMPACT.

How it works

From first call to recap

01

First conversation

We get to know your community, your audience and your ambition, and agree together what the experience should deliver.

02

Programme design

We choose the foundations, the medium and the experts, and build the full day programme around them.

03

Delivery

IMPACT is on the floor with coaches and experts. The host provides the venue, the reach and the logistics.

04

Recap & opvolging

Photo, video and a recap for both sides — and where possible a follow-up for the young people.

What each side brings

The host brings

  • Ticketing and registrations
  • Communication to their own community
  • Marketing and reach
  • Venue and practical organisation
  • The main programme and any extras

IMPACT brings

  • The six foundations as a method
  • A programme built for the audience
  • Our own team, experts and coaches
  • Experience design and guidance on the floor
  • Works on a fixed fee
