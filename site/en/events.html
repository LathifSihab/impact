Bespoke

Days, Camps, Retreats and Community are our four pillars. Hosted Experiences is not a fifth pillar, but another way those formats can be organised and offered — with a host who is the face of the experience.

Format 01

IMPACT [Camps]

Multi-day, intensive experiences where young people work on teamwork, discipline, mindset and movement through one central medium — basketball today, something else tomorrow. The medium creates the context; the foundations set the content.

  • Multi-day, with overnight stays
  • A fixed expert or coach per foundation
  • Mainly ages 8–14 and 14–18
  • Aftermovie and recap afterwards in the Journal

Future Camps can be built around a completely different medium — a Theater Edition, a Multisport Edition, or another sport, discipline or interest that brings young people together. We do not run a basketball camp or a theatre camp with a few workshops bolted on: we build an IMPACT experience in which the medium is one of the ways to make our foundations tangible.

See Basketball Edition 2027
Format 02

IMPACT [Days]

Short, powerful and accessible experiences that introduce young people to the vision and the foundations of IMPACT: from workshops and school days to talks, community events and hosted experiences.

  • One day, at our venue or yours
  • Or a longer programme across several sessions
  • Modular: from a single workshop to a full programme
  • Mainly ages 14–18 and school groups

A Day needs no overarching medium such as sport — the foundations are the starting point. Schools can book us for a single Day or for a programme across several sessions; depending on the needs, the age and the themes, we put the programme together and bring in the right experts from our pool.

Request a Day
Format 03

IMPACT [Retreats]

Guided programmes for student entrepreneurs and young founders around entrepreneurship, personal leadership and purpose. Smaller groups, over a longer period.

  • A programme rather than a one-off activation
  • ages 18–25
  • Focused on ownership, direction and network
  • A limited number of places per edition
Put me on the waiting list
Format 04

IMPACT [Community]

An IMPACT experience does not stop on the last day. In the community, young people stay connected with coaches, experts and each other — online and offline.

  • Contact with each other, coaches, experts and inspiring people
  • Knowledge, experience and opportunities
  • Online and offline, year-round
  • In time, the thread that connects every format

Community, to us, is more than a list of people who receive our newsletter. It is the environment where young people stay connected in an accessible way — including between two activations.
