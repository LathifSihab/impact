IMPACT for all

Talent is everywhere. Opportunity isn't.

We do not want to run experiences only for young people who can pay for a ticket themselves. Through partnerships, sponsorship and other income we build a fund to support those for whom these opportunities are less obvious, financially or socially.

How it works

Not one-off participation, but something that lasts

01

Building the fund

Partnerships, sponsorship and contributions together form the fund that pays for places.

02

Giving a place

Young people take part in a Camp, Day or Retreat without the cost being a barrier.

03

Making what comes next possible

We deliberately spend part of the fund outside our own activations — a club membership, for example, or equipment.

A young person who discovers during an IMPACT activation that a sport really suits them, but does not have the means at home to join a club — that is what IMPACT FOR ALL exists to answer.

So the question is not only “how do we get this young person to take part once?”, but also “how can that experience lead to something lasting?”. Support can mean taking part in an activation, a ticket, a club membership, equipment or other opportunities that contribute to their development. The exact structure of the fund is still being worked out.

Fund a ticket

Make a financial contribution

04

Open up places as a partner

As a partner, open up a fixed number of places a year for young people who would not otherwise get one.
