Experts & coaches

Help build one of our foundations

Our activations are carried by people who are experts in their own field. We are building a standing pool of IMPACT experts, each tied to one or more foundations.

01

Who we are looking for

Coaches, therapists, dietitians, mental performance experts, entrepreneurs and sports instructors experienced in working with young people.

02

What you do

You design and lead sessions inside a Camp, Day or Retreat, each starting from one of the six foundations.

03

How it works

An introduction, a trial session inside an activation, and then a place in the standing expert pool.
