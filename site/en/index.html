The foundations

Six foundations. One stronger generation.

Every Camp, every Day and every Retreat is built from these six foundations. The medium changes. The foundations stay.

How they come to life, and for which age, differs per activation — through sport, community, retreats or workshops.

[01] Self-knowledge

The better young people understand themselves, the better they handle choices, emotions, energy and pressure.

[02] Healthy habits & lifestyle

Sleep, food, movement and recovery decide how young people feel, function and perform.

[03] Resilience

Growing up brings pressure and uncertainty. We teach young people how to carry themselves through difficult moments.

[04] Ownership

Taking responsibility for yourself, your choices and your growth — from awareness and independence.

[05] Human connection

In a world that keeps getting more digital, we believe strongly in the power of real human connection.

[06] Talent & passion

Room to discover talents and passions, without the pressure of already knowing who you are.

Our formats

The medium changes. The foundations stay.

Explore our formats

01 IMPACT [Days]

Short, powerful experiences where the foundations themselves are the starting point. For schools, among others.

One day 02 IMPACT [Camps]

Multi-day experiences where young people work intensively on teamwork, discipline, mindset and movement through one central medium.

Multi-day 03 IMPACT [Retreats]

Guided programmes for student entrepreneurs around entrepreneurship, personal leadership and purpose.

Programme 04 IMPACT [Community]

An online and offline community connecting young people with coaches, experts and each other.

Ongoing — IMPACT [Hosted]

Days and Camps we build together with an external club, organisation or host. They host, IMPACT builds the experience.

IMPACT for all

Talent is everywhere. Opportunity isn't.

We want to avoid quality experiences and development opportunities being available only to young people whose parents can pay for them. Through partnerships, sponsorship and contributions we build a fund to support young people — during and after an IMPACT activation.
