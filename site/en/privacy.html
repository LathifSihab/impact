Privacy statement

How we handle the data of participants, parents, partners and visitors.

Not final yet. The text below is a structure for IMPACT to complete and confirm with its own lawyer or accountant. Everything between [ ] must be replaced. Do not publish this page on the final domain before that review has happened.

Who processes your data

Legal hello@wemakeimpact.be.

What data, and why

  • Waiting list for an edition — the participant's first name and age, the parent or guardian's email address, and optionally the municipality. We use these to let you know when registration for that edition opens, and to check the age group fits. Basis: consent.
  • Newsletter . Unsubscribe with one click in any email.
  • Contact and partnership enquiries — name, email address and whatever you write yourself. Basis: answering your question.
  • Where your sign-up came from — the page and the campaign you arrived through. This is attached to your sign-up so we know what works. No profile is built about you.

Minors' data

For our camps and days we collect data about children and young people. We always do so through the parent or guardian: the email address you leave is the adult’s, and that adult gives consent.
