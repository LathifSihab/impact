What it is

Five days where basketball is the setting and development is the goal

The medium creates a context where young people come together, have fun and can develop. At the same time we use that environment to bring our broader IMPACT foundations to life. Alongside basketball we deliberately build in sessions on movement, mental skills, relaxation, communication, connection and healthy habits.
