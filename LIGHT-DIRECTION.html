Design direction · site/ · September 2026

Let the page breathe

A light, spacious rebuild of the IMPACT demo site — same content, same nine sections, none of the black grounds. Every number below is a delta against what is in site/assets/css/style.css today.

Benchmark
mirterens.com — airy grid, full-bleed video header
Content cut
None. Density is solved with space, not deletion
Black grounds today
7 across 5 pages, plus the utility bar and footer
Touched files
style.css tokens, main.js nav observer, each page shell

01What is actually making it feel heavy

Worth naming precisely, because three of the four causes are single-token fixes rather than a redesign. The pages are not over-written — they are under-spaced and over-contrasted.

Sections are 120px tall on the inside, and alternate ground colour every single time--pad:120px with .section--sand on nearly every other block. The eye reads each colour flip as a hard edge, so nine sections feel like nine separate pages stacked up.
Section heads sit 56px from their content.sec-head{margin-bottom:56px} — barely more than a paragraph gap, so headline and grid read as one dense mass instead of a title and its contents.
Dark grounds carry long-form copy.section--black on the homepage formats block, over.html, events.html and samenwerken.html. Body text at rgba(255,255,255,.62) on #0F1015 is the single biggest source of the "intense" read.
The page opens on a black utility bar.util is var(--black) and sits above the nav, so the first 40px of every page is a dark band before the hero has even started.
The hero is a fixed 760px box under an opaque white nav.hero{height:760px} with .nav{background:var(--white);position:sticky}. The video never reaches the top of the screen, and the nav is a permanent white bar cutting across it.

02One vertical rhythm, six steps

mirterens holds a very small set of spacing values and uses the biggest one far more often than feels comfortable. Replace the ad-hoc margins in style.css with these six tokens and use nothing in between.

--sp-18px
label to the thing it labels · icon gaps
--sp-220px
paragraph to paragraph · list items
--sp-340px
grid gap — up from the current 24–32px
--sp-488px
section head to section content — replaces .sec-head's 56px
--sp-5clamp(96px, 13vw, 176px)
the new --pad. Section top and bottom, every section
--sp-6clamp(140px, 18vw, 240px)
before and after a full-bleed moment — the reel, the newsletter dome, the footer
The rule that does the most work

Where two sections share the same ground colour, the padding does not double — collapse to a single --sp-5 between them. That is what stops an airy page from turning into an endless one.

03Hierarchy: three widths, not one

Right now almost everything runs to the full --maxw:1296px. Giving each kind of content its own width is what reads as "considered" without changing a single word.

RoleWidthWhere it applies
Full bleed100vwHero video, the feedback reel, image bands. Edge to edge, no gutter.
Grid1296pxEvent cards, format cards, team, partners — unchanged.
Reading62–66chEvery .intro and .body paragraph. Currently these stretch to 1296px and are the hardest thing on the page to read.

Also: raise the section-head margin from 56px to --sp-4, and move .sec-head p off text-align:right — the ragged right edge against a right-aligned block is a second thing for the eye to resolve.

05The light palette

Drop-in replacements for the :root block in style.css. The reds are untouched — they are the brand and they carry all the emphasis now that nothing else is dark.

Paper

#FBFAF7 · --paper

Default page ground. Warmer and two steps lighter than the current --bone.

Raised

#FFFFFF · --raised

Cards, the waitlist panel, dropdowns. White is now the lifted surface, not the ground.

Recess

#F1EEE7 · --recess

Alternating bands and the footer. Replaces every --sand and every --black ground.

Ink

#1D1B18 · --ink

Type only — never a ground. Warmed off the current #0F1015 so it sits on paper without ringing.

Muted

#6E6A62 · --muted

Body copy and standfirsts. 6.1:1 on paper.

Hairline

#E5E1D8 · --line

Does the work the black grounds used to do: separates without shouting.

Red

#CA0013 · --red

Unchanged. Primary CTA, the fundamenten ground, and nothing else.

Burgundy

#560216 · --burgundy

Demote to CTA cards only. Retire it as a full-section ground on social-impact and samenwerken.

Keep

  • Archivo Black uppercase display. On a light ground it reads as confident rather than aggressive — it needs the space more than it needs the dark.
  • Round pills, square everything else. The existing rule is good and distinctive; don't add radius elsewhere.
  • The red. With no black competing, one accent carries the whole page.

Retire

  • .section--black entirely. Delete the class rather than leaving it available.
  • The black utility bar. Fold Brochure / Media / Journal / Contact into the nav overlay and the footer; the language switch moves into the nav.
  • The black mobile menu. Paper ground, ink type, hairline dividers — the same menu, unlit.
  • The black footer. --recess with a hairline top edge.

06Full-screen video hero and overlay nav

The pieces already exist — site/assets/video/hero.mp4 and .webm ship, and main.js already attaches the video progressively. Three changes turn it into the mirterens-style header.

A · The hero fills the viewport
/* replaces .hero{height:760px;background:var(--black)} */
.hero{
  position:relative;
  height:100svh;              /* svh, not vh — no jump when mobile chrome hides */
  min-height:560px;
  overflow:hidden;
  background:var(--recess);   /* light, so a slow video never flashes black */
}
.hero video,.hero > picture img{
  position:absolute;inset:0;width:100%;height:100%;
  object-fit:cover;
}
/* Scrim only where type sits. A full-frame overlay is what made the old
   hero feel heavy — this darkens the bottom third and leaves the rest clean. */
.hero::after{
  content:"";position:absolute;inset:0;z-index:2;pointer-events:none;
  background:linear-gradient(to top,
    rgba(20,18,16,.62) 0%,
    rgba(20,18,16,.20) 42%,
    rgba(20,18,16,0)   72%);
}
.hero-content{
  position:relative;z-index:3;height:100%;
  display:flex;flex-direction:column;justify-content:flex-end;
  padding-bottom:clamp(56px,8vh,104px);
}

Keep the <picture> poster underneath — it is what renders under prefers-reduced-motion, on Save-Data, and before the video decodes. The loadeddata → .is-ready fade in main.js already handles the handoff.

B · The nav overlays, then scrolls away
.nav{
  position:absolute;top:0;left:0;right:0;z-index:60;
  background:transparent;
  border-bottom:1px solid rgba(255,255,255,.16);
  transition:background 320ms ease, border-color 320ms ease,
             transform 320ms ease;
}
/* over the video: white marks, no plate */
.nav a,.nav .top{color:#fff}
.burger span,.burger span::before,.burger span::after{background:#fff}
.nav .logo img{filter:brightness(0) invert(1)}

/* past the hero: becomes a real sticky bar on paper */
html.is-past-hero .nav{
  position:fixed;
  background:var(--paper);
  border-color:var(--line);
  box-shadow:0 14px 34px -26px rgba(29,27,24,.28);
}
html.is-past-hero .nav a{color:var(--ink)}
html.is-past-hero .nav .logo img{filter:none}

/* and hides on scroll-down, returns on scroll-up */
html.is-past-hero.is-scrolling-down .nav{transform:translateY(-100%)}

@media (prefers-reduced-motion:reduce){
  .nav{transition:none}
  html.is-past-hero.is-scrolling-down .nav{transform:none}
}

Two flags instead of the current single is-scrolled: is-past-hero from an IntersectionObserver on the hero's last 80px (cheaper and more accurate than a scroll threshold), and is-scrolling-down from the existing scroll handler, keeping its hysteresis band so it cannot flap on a trackpad. Keep publishing --nav-h — the event page metabar and every [id] scroll offset read it.

C · The inner-page heroes

.hero--page currently runs a .84-opacity scrim to make long copy legible over bright frames. Under the light direction it should not be a dark hero at all: use a 60svh image band with the headline set below it on paper. That removes six scrims across the site in one change and is closer to what mirterens does on everything except its front page.

Spacing tokens map to a custom scale rather than Tailwind's default: extend spacing with section: clamp(96px,13vw,176px) and beat: clamp(140px,18vw,240px), then py-section / py-beat read as the rhythm rather than as arbitrary numbers.

07Order to do it in

Sequenced so the page looks better after each step and nothing is left half-converted overnight.
